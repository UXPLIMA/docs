## What You'll Do

1. Open the claim menu
2. Click "Claim This Chunk"
3. Name your claim
4. See the protection in action

**Time needed:** About 2 minutes

---

## Step 1: Stand Where You Want to Claim

Go to the location you want to protect. This is usually where you've built something or where you plan to build.

<Callout type="tip" title="Choose Wisely!">

You're claiming a "chunk" - a 16x16 block area that extends from bedrock to sky. Stand somewhere in the middle of your build.

</Callout>

---

## Step 2: Open the Claim Menu

Type this command in chat:

```
/claim
```

A menu will open with several options:

| Button                   | What It Does                             |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| 📄 **My Claims**         | Shows all claims you own (empty for now) |
| 🌿 **Claim This Chunk**  | Creates a new claim here                 |
| 🔵 **Visualize Borders** | Shows claim boundaries with particles    |
| 📜 **Invite Inbox**      | Shows invitations from other players     |

---

## Step 3: Click "Claim This Chunk"

Click the **green grass block** labeled "Claim This Chunk".

You'll see:

- The cost (if economy is enabled)
- Confirmation button

Click to confirm!

---

## Step 4: Name Your Claim

A text input will appear. Type a name for your claim, for example:

```
My Castle
```

<Callout type="note" title="Naming Rules">

- At least 3 characters
- Letters, numbers, and spaces are allowed
- Special characters like emojis work too!

</Callout>

---


## Step 5: Congratulations! 🎉

Your claim is created! You'll see a success message:

> ✅ Claim "My Castle" has been successfully created!

---

## What Happens Now?

Your claimed chunk is now protected:

| Action         | Can You Do It? | Can Strangers Do It?   |
|----------------|----------------|------------------------|
| Break blocks   | ✅ Yes          | ❌ No                   |
| Place blocks   | ✅ Yes          | ❌ No                   |
| Open chests    | ✅ Yes          | ❌ No                   |
| Kill animals   | ✅ Yes          | ❌ No                   |
| Enter the area | ✅ Yes          | ⚠️ Depends on settings |

---

## See Your Claim Boundaries

Want to see exactly where your claim starts and ends?

1. Type `/claim`
2. Click **"Visualize Borders"** (the sea lantern)

You'll see glowing particles showing:

- 🟢 **Green** = Your claims
- 🔴 **Red** = Other players' claims
- 🔵 **Blue** = Unclaimed wilderness

---

## Manage Your Claim

Type `/claim` again and click **"My Claims"** to see your claim.

Click on your claim to open the management menu where you can:

- 📍 Set spawn point
- 👥 Add members
- 🚩 Change flags (rules)
- 🗺️ View the map
- ⏰ Extend claim time
- 📦 Access the vault
- 🗑️ Delete the claim
