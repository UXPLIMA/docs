uxmCosmetics ships 234 pieces. Every one is an entry in its family's file: change it, remove it or add your own,
and run `/uxmcosmetics reload`. The name and sentence of each shipped piece are also in the messages files under
`cosmetic.<family>.<id>`, so each language names it in its own words.

| Family | Pieces | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [Trails](trail.md) | 23 | Particles behind a walking player. |
| [Halos](halo.md) | 15 | A ring of particles above the head. |
| [Cloaks](cloak.md) | 13 | A cape or a pair of wings drawn behind the player. |
| [Hats](hat.md) | 20 | An item worn on the head. |
| [Suits](suit.md) | 4 | Coloured or modelled armour. |
| [Emotes](emote.md) | 20 | A face over the head, a burst and a sound, played on click. |
| [Balloons](balloon.md) | 27 | A block on a lead, floating beside the player. |
| [Gadgets](gadget.md) | 44 | A hotbar item that does something on right click. |
| [Pets](pet.md) | 19 | An entity that walks after the player. |
| [Mounts](mount.md) | 3 | An entity the player rides. |
| [Morphs](morph.md) | 20 | The player is hidden and an entity stands in their place. |
| [Banners](banner.md) | 26 | A patterned banner worn on the head. |

The ids are what `/cosmetics equip`, `/uxmcosmetics grant`, a box table, a node and the placeholders use.
