An entity the player rides. `categories/mount.conf` ships 3 pieces. The node of each is `uxmcosmetics.mount.<id>`. Prices are in
`economy.currency`, experience points by default.

| Id | Name | Rarity | Price | Entity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `infernal_horror` | Infernal Horror | legendary | 5,500 | `SKELETON_HORSE` |
| `nyan_sheep` | Nyan Sheep | rare | 2,200 | `SHEEP` |
| `glacial_steed` | Glacial Steed | epic | 4,000 | `HORSE` |

How the family behaves and every key it takes: [Category files](../config/category-files.md).
