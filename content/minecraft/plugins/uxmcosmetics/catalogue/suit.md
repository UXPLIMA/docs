Coloured or modelled armour. `categories/suit.conf` ships 4 pieces. The node of each is `uxmcosmetics.suit.<id>`. Prices are in
`economy.currency`, experience points by default.

| Id | Name | Rarity | Price | Pieces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rainbow_suit` | Rainbow Suit | legendary | 5,000 | LEATHER_HELMET, LEATHER_CHESTPLATE, LEATHER_LEGGINGS, LEATHER_BOOTS |
| `ocean_suit` | Ocean Suit | rare | 1,000 | LEATHER_HELMET, LEATHER_CHESTPLATE, LEATHER_LEGGINGS, LEATHER_BOOTS |
| `crimson_suit` | Crimson Suit | rare | 1,000 | LEATHER_HELMET, LEATHER_CHESTPLATE, LEATHER_LEGGINGS, LEATHER_BOOTS |
| `gold_suit` | Gold Suit | epic | 2,200 | GOLDEN_HELMET, GOLDEN_CHESTPLATE, GOLDEN_LEGGINGS, GOLDEN_BOOTS |
