With PlaceholderAPI installed, uxmCosmetics publishes the [`%uxmcosmetics_...%` placeholders](../placeholders/reference.md)
and `/uxmcosmetics doctor` says `published`. Without it the rest of the plugin works and the doctor warns.

Related: [Placeholders](../placeholders/reference.md)
