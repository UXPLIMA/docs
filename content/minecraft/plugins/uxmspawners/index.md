uxmSpawners replaces the vanilla spawner with one a player can manage. It keeps what it produces
in its own storage instead of dropping it on the floor, kills the mobs it spawns so nobody has to
stand there doing it, and sells the result from a menu at prices you set or that your shop plugin
already knows.

Every part of it is a module in its own file, so you can run it as a plain drop-in replacement or
as a full economy sink.
