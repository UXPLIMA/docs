uxmSpawners replaces the vanilla spawner with one a player owns and manages from a menu. It keeps
what it produces in its own storage instead of dropping it on the floor, can kill its own mobs so
nobody has to stand next to it, and sells the result at prices you set or that your shop plugin
already knows. Stacking identical spawners into one raises its level and multiplies its output.
