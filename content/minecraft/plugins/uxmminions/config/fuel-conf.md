Each block under `tiers` is a grade: `item`, `speed` (0 to 90 percent off the action time) and `actions`.

| Grade | `item` | `speed` | `actions` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `coal` | `COAL` | 10 | 32 |
| `charcoal` | `CHARCOAL` | 10 | 24 |
| `coal-block` | `COAL_BLOCK` | 20 | 320 |
| `blaze-rod` | `BLAZE_ROD` | 35 | 200 |
| `lava-bucket` | `LAVA_BUCKET` | 40 | 400 |
| `nether-star` | `NETHER_STAR` | 60 | 2000 |
