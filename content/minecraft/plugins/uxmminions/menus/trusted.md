| Slot | Item | Click |
|---|---|---|
| 10 to 16 | Each trusted player's head | Takes them off |
| 27 | Arrow, back | The panel |

Adding is `/minions trust <id> <player>`, because a name is typed. List source `minions:trusted`; action
`minions:untrust`.
