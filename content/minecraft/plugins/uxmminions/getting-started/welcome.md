uxmMinions lets players place small workers that mine, chop, farm, fish, fight, collect, feed, sell, drive spawners or
heal on their own. Every minion type is a file, every level of it is a record of numbers, and the output goes into the
minion's own storage, a nearby chest, or straight to a sale.

A minion here works only while its owner is on the server and only while its chunk is already loaded. It is a presence
multiplier, not an income stream: nothing is accrued for the time in between, and there is no key that changes that.
