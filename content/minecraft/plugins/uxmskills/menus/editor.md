## Editor

The folders in 10 to 16 (`skills:editor-folders`); a click opens one (`skills:editor-folder`). The words button opens
the languages (`skills:editor-words`).

## Files

The files of a folder (`skills:editor-files`); a click opens one (`skills:editor-file`). `skills:editor-back-folders`
goes back.

## Keys

The keys of a file (`skills:editor-keys`); a click asks for a new value (`skills:editor-value`).
`skills:editor-back-files` goes back.

## Words

The languages (`skills:editor-languages`, `skills:editor-language`), then the lines of one (`skills:editor-lines`); a
click asks for the new wording (`skills:editor-reword`). `skills:editor-back-languages` goes back. `skills:editor-empty`
shows when there is nothing to change.
