uxmSkills gives players skills that level as they play. Eleven ship as examples: mining, excavation, woodcutting,
farming, fishing, combat, archery, defence, agility, alchemy and enchanting. Levels pay stat points, open abilities and
can drop loot, while an anti-farm layer keeps repeated work from paying in full forever. Every skill, source, ability,
stat and window is a file.

## What it does

| Part | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [Skills](../features/skills.md) | One file per skill: curve, stat points, abilities, sources, conditions, capstone, gates, loot |
| [Curves and formulas](../features/curves.md) | Presets, formulas with your own variables, or lists |
| [Sources](../features/sources.md) | The price list: seventeen types of work and what each pays |
| [Abilities](../features/abilities.md) | Twenty eight shipped, tuned per file, opened by level or bought with points |
| [Stats and resources](../features/stats.md) | Six stats with traits, and mana and stamina |
| [Experience and boosts](../features/experience.md) | Multipliers, rank nodes, a calendar of boosts, marked items, land |
| [Anti-farm](../features/anti-farm.md) | Placed block marks, heat maps, pressure, stillness, and terms that pull back up |
| [Progress and rewards](../features/progress.md) | Notifications, level up, capstones, specialisation, death |
| [Leaderboards](../features/leaderboards.md) | Every skill, total and average, all time, monthly, weekly and daily |
| [Data and backups](../features/data.md) | Backups, restore, AuraSkills import, recalculation, diagnostics |
| [The editor](../features/editor.md) | Content files and message lines changed in game |
