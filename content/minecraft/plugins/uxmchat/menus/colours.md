`/chatcolor`, node `uxmchat.colour`. Six rows.

| Slots | Holds | Click |
|---|---|---|
| 10 to 16, 19 to 25, 28 to 34 | The presets, each as its dye, with a preview in your name; the one in use glows | Uses the colour, or says it is locked |
| 38 to 42 | The styles: bold, italic, underlined, struck through, scrambled; one that is on glows | Turns the style on or off |
| 49 | A barrier | Plain words: no colour and no style |
| 48, 50 | Arrows | The page |

A preset reads `in use`, `yours` or `locked`.
