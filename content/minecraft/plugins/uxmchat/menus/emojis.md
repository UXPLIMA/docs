`/emoji`, node `uxmchat.emoji`. Six rows; the emoji sit as paper in slots 10 to 16, 19 to 25, 28 to 34 and 37 to 43,
each showing its glyph and shortcuts. Arrows at 48 and 50.

A click closes the window and sends the emoji's shortcut as a line that copies itself. An emoji whose node the player
lacks is not listed.
