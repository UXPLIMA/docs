`/friend`, node `uxmchat.friend`. Six rows; friends sit as player heads in slots 10 to 16, 19 to 25, 28 to 34 and 37 to
43, favourites first, with the server each is on. Arrows at 48 and 50.

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| Left | Asks for a line and sends it to the friend in private |
| Right | Pins the friend to the top, or unpins them |
