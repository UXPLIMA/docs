`/recent`, node `uxmchat.recent`. Three rows; the last five lines said to you sit in slots 11 to 15, as paper.

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| Left | Asks for a line and sends it in private to whoever said the clicked one |
| Right | Ignores whoever said it |
