uxmDiscordSync connects a Paper server to a Discord server through a bot that runs inside the
plugin. A player links the two accounts once; after that their LuckPerms groups become Discord roles,
their nickname follows them, their Discord activity earns rewards in game, and a login can be
approved from their phone before they can move.

Every feature has its own switch, so it can be nothing more than a link button or the whole
connection between your game and your community. Several servers can share one bot through Redis.

These pages describe uxmDiscordSync 2.2.0. Coming from 2.1.x, read
[Upgrading to 2.2.0](getting-started/upgrading.md) first.
