uxmDiscordSync connects a Minecraft server to a Discord guild in both directions. Players link the
two accounts once; after that their ranks follow them, their nickname follows them, punishments
apply on both sides, and a login can be approved from a phone before the account is let in.

The bot is embedded: there is no second process to host. Every feature is off by default and turned
on in its own file, so you can run it as nothing but a link command or as the whole community
backbone.
