Guard protects areas of your worlds. A region says who may do what inside it (break, place, open, hurt, use
items, walk in or out) and what nature may do there (fire, liquids, explosions, growth, mobs). It can also
change the players inside it: flight, game mode, messages on the way in, healing, blocked commands. Guard is a
module of uxmEssentials: no second plugin, no WorldGuard and no WorldEdit needed, and it runs the same on
Paper and Folia.
