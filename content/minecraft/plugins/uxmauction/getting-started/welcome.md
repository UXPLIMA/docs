uxmAuction is an auction house. Players list items at a fixed price or for bids, place buy orders the house fills from
new listings, watch for the items they want, and collect what they are owed from a mailbox. It prices in several
currencies at once and never converts between them.

## What it does

| Part | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [Selling](../features/selling.md) | Fixed-price listings, whole or in parts, from a command or a window |
| [Bidding](../features/bidding.md) | Auctions with a bid step and anti-sniping |
| [Buy orders](../features/orders.md) | The house buys for a player and holds their money until it does |
| [Watches](../features/watches.md) | Being told when the item you want is listed |
| [The mailbox](../features/mailbox.md) | Everything the house owes a player, until they take it |
| [Fees](../features/fees.md) | Listing fees, reprice fees, sale tax, purchase tax, per group |
| [Currencies](../features/currencies.md) | Nine kinds, from Vault to an item to any plugin described by hand |
| [Categories](../features/categories.md) | The tabs of the house, by item id pattern |
| [History](../features/history.md) | What an item has sold for |
| [Notices](../features/notices.md) | Effects for every moment, a Discord webhook, events for other plugins |
| [Operators](../features/operators.md) | Lock the house, look up a listing, read the log, restore, return, purge |
| [A network](../features/network.md) | One house across every server on a shared database |

Every window is a file, every command can be renamed, every line a player reads is in a language file. Seven languages
ship.
