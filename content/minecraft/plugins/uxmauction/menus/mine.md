Three windows with the same bottom row, so they read as three tabs:

| Slot | Tab | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| 47 | Writable book | Your listings |
| 48 | Gold ingot | Your bids |
| 49 | Hopper | Your orders |
| 51 | Feather | The house |

## Your listings

`/auction mine`. A left click opens a listing; a right click takes it back.

## Bids

`/auction bids`. The auctions you lead; a click opens one.

## Orders

`/auction orders`. Your open buy orders; a click gives the money of one back.
