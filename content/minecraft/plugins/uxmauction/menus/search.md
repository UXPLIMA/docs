## Search

`/auction search <text>` or the sign of the house. The shelf of listings whose names hold the words: a spyglass at 46
shows what was asked, a feather at 47 goes back to the whole house, the hopper at 48 sorts.

## History

`/auction history`. What the item in your hand has sold for, newest first; the spyglass at 46 shows the item, the
feather at 47 goes home.

## Profile

`/auction profile [player]`. A seller's shelf; their head at 46 shows who and what they earned, the feather at 47 goes
home, the hopper at 48 sorts.
