A click on any listing. Three rows; the item sits in the middle, slot 13.

| Listing | Slot 11 | Slot 15 |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed price | Emerald block: buy all, with the price and what is left | Emerald: buy one, with the price each (retail) |
| Auction | Gold ingot: bid the next step, with the offer, the leading bid, the leader and the time | |
| Your own | | Paper: what it is and how it stands |

A feather at 22 goes back to the house. The bid button offers the next step, which the house works out from the leading
bid and the step of the currency. A file may write `auction:buy:<number>` for a button that buys that many.
