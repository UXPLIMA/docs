## Claims

Claims in 10 to 16, 19 to 25, 28 to 34 and 37 to 43. Left click opens one (`claims:open-claim`), right click teleports
(`claims:tp`).

| Slot | Button |
|---|---|
| 2 | Mine (`claims:list-mine`) |
| 3 | Claims I am in (`claims:list-member`) |
| 5 | Public warps (`claims:list-warps`) |
| 6 | Favourites (`claims:list-favourites`) |
| 45 | Map |
| 46 | Market |
| 53 | Inbox |

A row shows size, world, members and your role.

## Map

Slots 0 to 44, one chunk each, the one you stand in glowing. A row shows state, claim, owner and price. A click on a
free chunk claims it; on your own or a member claim, opens it (`claims:cell`).

## Market

Every claim for sale and sub area for rent: kind, owner, price, size and days. Left click buys or rents
(`claims:listing-take`), right click goes to look (`claims:listing-visit`).

## Inbox

What happened while you were away. The cauldron at 45 clears it (`claims:inbox-clear`).
