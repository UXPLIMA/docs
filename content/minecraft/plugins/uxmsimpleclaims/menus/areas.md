## Sub areas

Every sub area with chunks, members, rent and tenant; a click opens it (`claims:area-open`). The emerald at 45 makes one
of the chunk you stand in (`claims:area-create`).

## Area

| Slot | Button | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | Add or remove the chunk you stand in | `claims:area-chunk` |
| 22 | Its flags | `claims:area-flags` |
| 24 | Offer for rent, with the rent and tenant | `claims:area-offer` |
| 31 | End the tenancy | `claims:area-end` |
| 40 | Delete | `claims:area-delete` |
