## Members

Members as heads, with role, since when, and when a timed membership ends.

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| Left | Promote (`claims:promote`) |
| Right | Demote (`claims:demote`) |
| Shift left | Kick (`claims:kick`) |
| Shift right | Ban (`claims:ban`) |

The emerald at 45 invites (`claims:invite`); the barrier at 53 opens the [bans](#bans).

## Bans

Banned players, by whom and until when; a click lifts the ban (`claims:unban`).

## Roles

Every role with priority, permission count and holders. Left click opens it (`claims:role-open`), right click deletes
a role of your own (`claims:role-delete`); the emerald at 45 creates one (`claims:role-create`).

## Role

Every role permission with its state; a click turns it on or off (`claims:permission-toggle`).
