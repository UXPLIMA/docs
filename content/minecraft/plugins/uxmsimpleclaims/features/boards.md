| Board | Ranks by |
|---|---|
| `chunks` | Chunks owned |
| `members` | Members over all of a player's claims |
| `visits` | Visits through a player's public warps |

All time only. `leaderboard.size` (10) is how many places they hold and `leaderboard.refresh-seconds` (120) how often
they are read. Read them with the [placeholders](../placeholders/reference.md#boards).

Related: [Placeholders](../placeholders/reference.md)
