uxmSimpleClaims protects land by the chunk. A player claims the chunk they stand in, grows the claim chunk by chunk or
by a square, and decides who may do what inside it. Everything a server owner might not want can be configured or
turned off.

## What it does

| Part | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [Worlds](../features/worlds.md) | Per world: normal, claims required, protected or left alone; heights; wilderness rules |
| [Claiming](../features/claiming.md) | Chunk by chunk, by radius or as you walk; merging, splitting, the map and borders |
| [Prices and limits](../features/prices-and-limits.md) | Four price curves, free chunks, refunds, and limit groups by permission |
| [Members and roles](../features/members-and-roles.md) | Invites, bans, timed memberships, and roles with thirty six permissions |
| [Flags and rules](../features/flags-and-rules.md) | Thirteen flags, locks, and whitelists or blacklists of blocks and mobs |
| [Sub areas and rent](../features/sub-areas.md) | Areas inside a claim with their own members, flags and rent |
| [Market and warps](../features/market-and-warps.md) | Selling claims, public warps with fees, favourites |
| [Upkeep](../features/upkeep.md) | Taxes, time limited claims and inactivity, each off by default |
| [Entering and teleports](../features/entering.md) | Titles, bars and greetings, and what other plugins' teleports may do |
| [Staff tools](../features/staff-tools.md) | Bypass, inspection, snapshots, the audit trail, the inbox |
| [Networks](../features/networks.md) | Servers sharing claims, Redis, cross server teleports |
| [Boards](../features/boards.md) | Chunks, members and visits boards |
