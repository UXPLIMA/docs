---
title: Your first claim
order: 204
description: Claim, grow, invite, set flags and open a warp, step by step.
icon: footprints
---

1. Stand where you want to live and type `/claim`. The chunk is yours, named `claim1`, and its edges glow for
   `display.border-seconds`.
2. Grow it: `/claim radius 2` claims the square of chunks two to each side, or `/claim auto` claims as you walk (run it
   again to stop).
3. Name it: `/claim rename Home`.
4. Invite a friend: `/claim invite Steve`. They type `/claim accept`. `/claim invite Steve 7d` makes the membership end
   by itself.
5. Promote them: `/claim role Steve moderator`.
6. Open `/claim` again while standing in the claim: the [claim window](../menus/claim.md) has members, roles, flags,
   rules, sub areas and history.
7. Turn PvP on from the flags window, or `/claim flag pvp true`.
8. Set a spawn with `/claim setspawn` and open a warp with `/claim warp open 50`: anybody can now
   `/claim tp Home`, paying you 50.

Related: [Claiming](../features/claiming.md), [Members and roles](../features/members-and-roles.md)
