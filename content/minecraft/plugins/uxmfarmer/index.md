uxmFarmer gives every claim or island one NPC that does the farming. It harvests crops as they
ripen, kills the animals you tell it to, collects the XP they drop, keeps everything in a storage
that grows as the player pays to upgrade it, and sells the result at your shop's prices minus a
tax you set.

It is built as five independent modules, each with its own permission and its own on/off switch,
so a server can ship it as a small quality-of-life feature or as the centre of its economy.
