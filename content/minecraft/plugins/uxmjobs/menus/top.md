`/jobs board` opens `menus/top.conf`: six rows, one player head per place, read from the cache.

| Slot | Item | Click |
|---|---|---|
| 10 to 16, 19 to 25, 28 to 34 | A place: the name and the level | |
| 48, 50 | Arrows | |
| 49 | Barrier, close | |

Tokens: `%top_name%`, `%top_level%` (the level in the job, or the total across every job). List source `jobs:top`.
