uxmJobs pays players for work: breaking, placing, killing, fishing, smelting, enchanting, trading and more, priced per
job in money, job experience and job points. Every job levels on its own curve. The defaults ship with protections
against farms and loops, a soft cap on income, and a report of how much money the plugin created.

## What it does

| Part | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [Jobs and prices](../features/jobs-and-prices.md) | One file per job: a curve, costs, bounds and a price table of behaviours and targets |
| [Action types](../features/action-types.md) | Thirty behaviours, from `mine` and `kill` to `enchant`, `trade` and `explore` |
| [Joining and leaving](../features/joining-and-leaving.md) | Slots, seats, fees, a leave cost, and an archive that remembers |
| [Payments](../features/payments.md) | One order for every multiplier, and `/jobs why` that explains a wage |
| [Protections](../features/protections.md) | A placed block ledger, decay, farm grades, claims and disabled worlds |
| [Income cap and tax](../features/income-cap-and-tax.md) | A soft ceiling per hour, a tax by node, and a server account that can run dry |
| [Boosters](../features/boosters.md) | Permission, timed and scheduled multipliers |
| [Contracts](../features/contracts.md) | Documents a player pays for, which pay more in set hours |
| [Rewards and titles](../features/rewards-and-titles.md) | What a level gives, and the title it carries |
| [Points and the shop](../features/points-and-shop.md) | A currency earned only from work and spent only in the shop |
| [Zones](../features/zones.md) | Boxes drawn with a wand where work is worth more, and blocks grow back |
| [Signs](../features/signs.md) | Join signs and top list signs |
| [Leaderboards](../features/leaderboards.md) | All time, monthly, weekly and daily boards per job and in total |
| [Display](../features/display.md) | The action bar, the progress bar, dialogs, level ups and milestones |
| [Economy](../features/economy.md) | Where wages go and what costs are taken in, with nine backends and your own |
| [Report and tools](../features/report-and-tools.md) | The payout report, income alerts, preview, inspect and prune |
| [In-game editor](../features/editor.md) | Make and change jobs from inside the game |
