uxmGlow gives a player an outline they choose and everybody can see, through walls and across a
lobby. A staff member in a crowd, a tournament team, the host of an event: all findable at a
glance, with nothing to hand out and nothing to wear.

A player opens `/glow`, clicks a colour, and glows in it. With one permission more they set
several colours instead of one and the outline steps through them. The choice survives a rejoin
and a restart, and it is checked against the permission every time it comes back, so a colour
that arrived with a rank leaves with the rank.

Every window is a file, every command can be renamed, and every line a player reads is in a
language file you can add to. The plugin needs nothing else installed: no economy, no
PlaceholderAPI, no permissions plugin beyond the one your server already runs.
