| Feature | How it works |
|---|---|
| Guestbook | A visitor signs the island's guestbook; the team pins, hides or deletes entries in the [guestbook window](../menus/travel.md#guestbook) |
| Ratings | A visitor rates an island from one to five with `/is rate` |
| Bookmarks | `/is bookmarks toggle` saves the island you stand on; the [bookmarks window](../menus/travel.md#bookmarks) takes you back |
| Alliances | Two islands ally with `/is alliance invite` and `accept`. Allies visit each other freely, may come onto a locked island, and share `/is allychat` |
| Island chat | `/is chat` switches your chat to your island's members; `/is chat <message>` sends one line. `uxmskyblock.chat.spy` and `/is spy` listen in |
| Activity | What happened on the island lately: bank moves, members, upgrades, boosters, missions and warps, filtered by member, kind and period |
| Trading | `/is trade <player>` opens one window for two players; each puts in what they offer and both agree before anything moves |

Ratings and the guestbook are in [social.conf](../config/social-conf.md), alliances in
[alliances.conf](../config/alliances-conf.md), chat in [chat.conf](../config/chat-conf.md) and trading in
[trade.conf](../config/trade-conf.md).
