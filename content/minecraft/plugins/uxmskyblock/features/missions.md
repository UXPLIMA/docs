## Missions

An island is given daily missions, weekly missions and one-time challenges, each a goal such as breaking blocks,
reaching a OneBlock phase or earning money, with a reward: money, crystals, items or commands. Progress counts for the
whole island. Missions open from the [missions window](../menus/economy.md#missions). The catalogue, the slots per day
and week, and the reset time zone are in [missions.conf](../config/missions-conf.md). A mission can be limited to some
game modes.

## Boosters

A booster multiplies spawners, crops, the ore generator, mob drops or experience on an island for a while. Boosters
are bought or given; `/is booster` shows those running. Staff grant one with `uxmskyblock.admin.booster`. See
[boosters.conf](../config/boosters-conf.md).

## The reward inbox

A reward a player could not be handed, because they were offline or their inventory was full, waits in their inbox.
`/is rewards` hands it over. See [rewards.conf](../config/rewards-conf.md).

## Seasons

A season runs for a while, the best islands on its boards are paid, and a new one starts. `/is season` shows the
season under way, `/is season top` its standings, and `/is season rewards` hands over what a season owes you. See
[seasons.conf](../config/seasons-conf.md).
