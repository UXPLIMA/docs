A word of a game mode answers only on an island of that mode, or where that mode's place is.

| Command | Mode | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `/is oneblock` | [OneBlock](../game-modes/oneblock.md) | Opens the [OneBlock window](../menus/game-modes.md#oneblock): the phase, its progress and the phases |
| `/is chunks` | [ChunkBlock](../game-modes/chunkblock.md) | Opens the [chunks window](../menus/game-modes.md#chunks): open chunks and the next one |
| `/is boxbuy` | [Boxed](../game-modes/boxed.md) | Buys growth for your box |
| `/is boxside [side]` | Boxed | Shows or chooses the side your box grows toward |
| `/is lore` | [Poseidon](../game-modes/poseidon.md) | Reads the lore of the sea |
| `/is plot time \| weather <setting>` | [Brix](../game-modes/brix.md) | Sets the time or weather of the plot you stand on |
| `/is plot reset [confirm]` | Brix | Clears everything built on your plot and lays its ground again |
| `/is plots` | Brix | Opens [every plot](../menus/game-modes.md#brix-plots), those in the contest first |
| `/is contest [join \| vote <stars>]` | Brix | Shows the contest, enters your plot, or votes for the plot you stand on |
| `/is course [season [number]]` | [Parkour](../game-modes/parkour.md) | Shows the best times on the course you stand on |
| `/is courses` | Parkour | Opens [every course](../menus/game-modes.md#parkour-courses), the most run first |
| `/is coursetag difficulty \| category [name]` | Parkour | Gives the course you stand on a difficulty or a category, or takes it off |
| `/is courserate <stars>` | Parkour | Rates the course you stand on, once you have finished it |
| `/is sail` | [TradeWinds](../game-modes/tradewinds.md) | Opens the [ports](../menus/game-modes.md#set-sail) your vessel can sail for |
| `/is market` | TradeWinds | Opens the market of the port your vessel lies in |
| `/is contracts` | TradeWinds | Takes and delivers that port's contracts |
| `/is cargo` | TradeWinds | Opens the cargo hold of the vessel you are aboard |
| `/is plunder` | TradeWinds | Takes part of the hold of the vessel you stand by, at sea |

In `/is plot time`, the words are `day`, `noon`, `sunset`, `night`, `midnight`, `sunrise`, `world` or a tick from 0 to
23999; in `/is plot weather`, `clear`, `rain` or `world`. These words stay English in every language.
