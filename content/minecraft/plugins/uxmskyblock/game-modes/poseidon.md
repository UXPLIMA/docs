A Poseidon island stands on the sea floor. Water is the air a Poseidon player breathes: in it they see and breathe,
out of it dry air and the sun burn them, and one who stops moving in the water drowns. The Nether of a Poseidon
island is flooded.

| Preset | The start |
|---|---|
| `poseidon` | The sea floor |
| `poseidon_ruin` | A shipwreck and a great ruin nearby |
| `poseidon_ruins` | Ringed by a shipwreck and old ruins |
| `poseidon_monument` | On a sunken temple of prismarine, with a door on every side |

`/is lore` opens the lore of the sea: a Java player reads a book, a Bedrock player a form. Its chapters open as the
team finishes the story missions. The Deep Elder, the island's boss, rises from the deep; its name and what is said
when it rises, is slain or sinks back are in the language files.

Breath, burning, drowning, the flooded Nether, the story missions and the boss are in
[poseidon.conf](../config/poseidon-conf.md). The boss drops from `loot/deep_boss.conf`.
