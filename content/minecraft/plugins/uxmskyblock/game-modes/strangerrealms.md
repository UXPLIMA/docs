A StrangerRealms island is real land, with an Upside Down in place of the Nether. The Upside Down is the island's
land mirrored block for block and distressed: the grass dead, the trees bare, the flowers withered. It is laid when
the island is made, in the world [dimensions.conf](../config/dimensions-conf.md) names for the Nether.

A compass, the Warped Compass, points at whoever stands on the other side of the veil.

Lights can glimmer across the veil, and switches used on one side act at the same place on the other. How the
Upside Down is distressed, the lights and the linked switches are in [strangerrealms.conf](../config/strangerrealms-conf.md).
Staff verb: `/isadmin realms remirror` lays the Upside Down again from the land.
