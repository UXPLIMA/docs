A CaveBlock island is enclosed in rock. Players arrive in a room carved out of it, with tunnels winding away and
ravines cut deep through it, and mine outward through rock and ores. The rock is closed in by a shell below, above and
on every side.

How deep and wide the rock is, the ores in it and how often, the room, the tunnels and the ravines are in
[caveblock.conf](../config/caveblock-conf.md).
