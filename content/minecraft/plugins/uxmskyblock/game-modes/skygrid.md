A SkyGrid island is a sparse grid of lone blocks hanging in the sky, one every few blocks along each axis. Players
arrive on a safe block at its centre and bridge from block to block. A chest in the grid holds a few of the chest
items, and a spawner raises one of its dimension's creatures.

The spacing, the blocks and their weights for the overworld, the Nether and the End, and the creatures a spawner may
raise are in [skygrid.conf](../config/skygrid-conf.md). Chests draw from the loot tables `loot/skygrid.conf`,
`loot/skygrid_nether.conf` and `loot/skygrid_end.conf`.
