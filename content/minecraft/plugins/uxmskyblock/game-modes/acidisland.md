An AcidIsland island stands in an acid sea. The sea burns whoever swims in it, and acid rain burns whoever it falls
on. Water taken from the sea is acid too: a bottle of it poisons the one who drinks it until it is made clean.

| Water | Clean or acid |
|---|---|
| The sea, and a bucket or bottle filled from it | Acid |
| Rain a cauldron catches | Clean |
| A cauldron a bucket of sea water was poured into | Acid, until it is emptied |
| A bottle smelted, or brewed with coal | Clean |

Drinking clean water gives back health. A vent in the sea takes an offering and gives something back, and an acid
tide can rise and fall, with a line to everyone on the island when it turns.
