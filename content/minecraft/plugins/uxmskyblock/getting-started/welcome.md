uxmSkyblock runs a skyblock server on Paper or Folia. Each player, alone or with a team, starts an island from a
preset, grows it and competes with the other islands on the server. The plugin is free and its source is public.

## What it does

| Area | What a player gets |
|---|---|
| Islands | An island from one of eighteen presets, a home, named homes, a border, a biome, a Nether and an End |
| Teams | Members, four roles and roles of your own, 35 permissions per role, invites, trust, bans |
| Money | A bank the team shares in up to nine currencies, interest, upkeep, and an island shop whose prices move |
| Progress | Seventeen upgrades with tiers, island level and worth, missions, boosters, seasons and leaderboards |
| Travel | Warps visitors open from a directory, visits with an optional fee, alliances and ally chat |
| Community | Ratings, a guestbook, bookmarks, an activity feed, island chat and player trading |
| Game modes | OneBlock, ChunkBlock, AcidIsland, CaveBlock, SkyGrid, Boxed, Poseidon, StrangerRealms, Brix, Parkour and TradeWinds |

## Who it is for

A server owner who wants a skyblock server that a team of staff can run without writing code: every window, line,
price and rule is in a file, and every line a player reads exists in seven languages. One server runs it alone;
several servers can share islands through one database and Redis.
