---
title: Your first island
order: 104
description: From /is to a team, a bank deposit and an upgrade.
icon: footprints
---

1. Type `/is`. A player with no island sees the [start window](../menus/island.md#start-an-island): one tile per
   preset. Right click a tile to look at it first, left click to start it.
2. You arrive on your island. `/is` now opens the [island menu](../menus/island.md#island-menu).
3. Type `/is invite <player>`. They have five minutes to type `/is accept`.
4. Type `/is bank deposit 1000` to put money in the bank the team shares.
5. Open **Upgrades** from the island menu and buy the next tier of island size.
6. Type `/is sethome mine` where you stand, and `/is gohome mine` later to return.
7. Type `/is level` to count your island's blocks, then `/is top` to see where it stands.

Every command is on [Commands](../commands/overview.md).
