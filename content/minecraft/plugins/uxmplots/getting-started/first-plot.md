## 1. Claim

Walk onto a free plot (its wall top is a stone slab) and type `/plot claim`, or `/plot auto` to be taken to the
nearest free one. The wall top turns to brick. `/plot price` says what the next one costs; the shipped world is free.

## 2. Trust a friend

```
/plot trust Alex
/plot trust Alex build,interact,container
```

Without grades a member may build and interact. `/plot members` opens the list, where each grade is a switch.

## 3. Shape it

```
/plot floor-list
/plot floor chequer
/plot name The Lighthouse
```

`/plot outline` draws the edge in the air. `/plot snapshot` keeps a picture you can put back.

## 4. Publish

```
/plot tag modern
/plot visiting open
```

It is in the gallery (`/plot browse`) from the first claim unless you `/plot hide` it. Visitors who stand in it for 30
seconds may rate it; the sign over the plot shows its name, owner, rating and visits.
