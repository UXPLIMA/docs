uxmPlots runs plot worlds for creative and building servers: a grid of plots and roads, claiming and trust, a gallery
with ratings and visits, guestbooks and tips, shared building sessions, contests with judging and a hall of fame, plot
levels, a cosmetic shop, renting and selling, snapshots, and protection against griefing and crash items.

## What it does

| Part | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [Plot worlds](../features/worlds.md) | One file per world: grid, layers, walls, roads, flags, entity caps, prices, looks |
| [Claiming](../features/claiming.md) | Claim, auto claim, limits by node, bought slots, homes and visiting |
| [Members and visitors](../features/members.md) | Four grades of trust, bans, who may visit, building sessions |
| [Flags](../features/flags.md) | Thirty four switches per plot, with world defaults and locks |
| [Protection](../features/protection.md) | Roads, forbidden blocks, cross-boundary tricks, lag machines, crash items, WorldEdit |
| [Shaping a plot](../features/shaping.md) | Floor, wall, border, ground, biome, clear, reset, snapshots, templates, merging |
| [Gallery](../features/gallery.md) | Ratings that resist friends, visits, trending, tags, pinned and sponsored rows |
| [Community](../features/community.md) | Guestbooks, tips, following builders, plot chat, reports |
| [Personality and shop](../features/personality-and-shop.md) | Music, ambience, time, weather and sign looks, and the shop that sells them |
| [Levels](../features/levels.md) | A level earned from what others did, unlocking plots, merges, entries and flags |
| [Market](../features/market.md) | Selling and renting plots |
| [Contests](../features/contests.md) | Phases from the clock, fresh or own plots, juries, rewards, the archive and hall of fame |
| [Boards and signs](../features/boards-and-signs.md) | Six boards, planted boards, the plot sign over every plot |
| [Expiry and staff](../features/expiry-and-staff.md) | Inactive plots, the report queue, the staff bench |
| [Network and Discord](../features/network-and-discord.md) | Several servers on one database, and webhook lines |
| [Currencies](../features/currencies.md) | Items, Vault, experience, Treasury or your own economy |
| [In-game editors](../features/editors.md) | A world editor, a contest editor, and how typing is asked for |
