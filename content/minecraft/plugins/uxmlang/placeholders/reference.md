uxmLang registers no PlaceholderAPI placeholders. A player's language shows in the window and in `/lang list`.

With PlaceholderAPI installed, the effect lines of [config.conf](../config/config-conf.md#effects) read other
plugins' placeholders, and `%mcmmo_level_<skill>%` is read off mcMMO itself.

Related: [config.conf](../config/config-conf.md#effects)
