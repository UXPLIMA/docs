PlaceholderAPI is needed. Every answer is read from memory; an unknown key or a request with no player answers
nothing.

| Placeholder | Gives |
|---|---|
| `%uxmshop_sections%` | How many catalogue sections there are |
| `%uxmshop_lines%` | How many lines can be traded |
| `%uxmshop_shops%` | How many player shops there are |
| `%uxmshop_my_shops%` | How many shops the reader owns |
| `%uxmshop_shop_limit%` | How many shops the reader may own; an offline player gets the default limit |

`/uxmshop doctor` says whether they were published. A section line's `conditions` read any PlaceholderAPI value, and a
`placeholder` currency reads a balance through one.
