uxmHeads draws players' faces in chat: eight rows of coloured characters, one per chat line, taken from the player's
skin. It sends a welcome with the face when a player joins and gives every other plugin the rows through
PlaceholderAPI.

| Part | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| Faces | The 8 by 8 face of any skin, with its hat layer, as eight chat rows |
| A welcome | A first-join and a join greeting with the face beside the words |
| Placeholders | Every row of the reader's face, any player's face, and the other player's face in a relational line |
| Bedrock | The face in the sixteen colours a Bedrock client has, picked by eye |
| Safety | Skins are fetched only from the hosts you list |
