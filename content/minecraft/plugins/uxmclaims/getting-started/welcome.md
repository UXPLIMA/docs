## What Does This Plugin Do?

Imagine you built an amazing castle in Minecraft. Without protection, anyone could:

- ❌ Break your walls
- ❌ Steal from your chests
- ❌ Blow it up with TNT
- ❌ Set it on fire

With uxmClaims:

- ✅ Only you (and people you trust) can modify your build
- ✅ Your chests are locked to outsiders
- ✅ TNT and creepers can't destroy anything
- ✅ Fire won't spread

---

## How Does It Work?

### Step 1: Player Claims a Chunk

A player stands on the land they want to protect and types `/claim`. They click "Claim This Chunk" and the land is now
theirs!

### Step 2: The Chunk is Protected

The claimed chunk (a 16x16 block area) is now protected. Nobody else can:

- Break or place blocks
- Open containers
- Damage animals
- Or do anything you haven't allowed!

### Step 3: Owner Manages the Claim

The owner can:

- Add friends as members
- Create custom roles
- Set specific rules (flags)
- Expand the claim to more chunks
