## How to Open

1. Open claim management
2. Click **Banned Players** (`banList`)

---

## What Is This Menu For?

Manage players who are not allowed in your claim. Banned players:

- Cannot enter your claim
- Cannot be invited to your claim
- Cannot interact with anything

---

## Menu Items

### `bannedHead` - Banned Player Entry

**Icon:** Player Head (shows their skin)

**What it shows:**

- Player name
- When they were banned

**Click to:** Unban the player

---

### `addBan` - Ban a Player

**Icon:** Lime Dye (or similar) ➕

**What it does:** Opens a text input to ban a player by name.

---

### `previousPage` & `nextPage` - Pagination

Navigate through pages if you have many bans.

---

### `back` - Back

Returns to claim management.

---

## How Banning Works

1. Click "Ban a Player"
2. Enter the player's name
3. They are now banned

**Banned players:**

- Are teleported out if inside the claim
- See a message that they're banned
- Cannot interact with the claim in any way

---

## Important Rules

- You **cannot ban current members** - Kick them first
- You **cannot ban yourself** (the owner)
- Ban limits may apply (default: 50 per claim)

---

## Unbanning

1. Find the player in the ban list
2. Click on their head
3. Confirm unbanning

They can now enter your claim again (but are not a member).
