- [Overview](overview.md)
- [Island Basics](island-basics.md) — The everyday commands for creating, reaching, and managing your own island.
- [Team & Roles](team-roles.md) — Everything for building and running your island team — members, invites, bans, and roles.
- [Warps](warps.md) — Public warps let other players teleport to specific spots on your island — a shop, a farm, an arena. An island can have several named warps.
- [Bank & Economy](bank.md) — Every island has a shared bank — a single balance the whole team can pay into. The bank is backed by Vault, so it uses your server's real economy.
- [Leaderboard](leaderboard.md) — Islands compete on a server-wide leaderboard, ranked by level (and points).
- [Admin](admin.md) — Tools for server staff. Everything here needs the skyblock.admin permission (default: op). To players without it, these sub-commands are hidden from tab completion and respond as unknown commands.
