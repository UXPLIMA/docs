## What It Shows

The menu lists public island warps. Clicking one teleports you to that warp,
respecting the island's visitor lock and ban list (you can't warp to an island
that's closed to you).

Warps are set by island members with `/is setwarp <name>` — see the
[Warps command page](../commands/warps.md).

---

## Customizing

Open `menus/warp.yml` to change the title, the item used for each warp, paging
arrows, and the back button. See [Menu Customization](../configuration/menus.md).
