## Minecraft Plugins

Premium plugins for Paper/Spigot servers.

- **[uxmAuction](minecraft/plugins/uxmauction/)** - An auction house with fixed prices, bids, buy orders and a mailbox for what players are owed
- **[uxmAuth](minecraft/plugins/uxmauth/)** - Login and registration for offline-mode servers and networks, on Paper, Folia and Velocity
- **[uxmBackup](minecraft/plugins/uxmbackup/)** - Backups taken from inside the server: worlds, files and databases into checked zip archives
- **[uxmChat](minecraft/plugins/uxmchat/)** - Chat for one server or a network: channels, formats, private messages, friends, tags and colours
- **[uxmCosmetics](minecraft/plugins/uxmcosmetics/)** - A wardrobe of cosmetics in twelve categories, from trails and pets to emotes and gadgets
- **[uxmCrates](minecraft/plugins/uxmcrates/)** - Crates whose odds a player can read, with reveal animations, keys, milestones and envoys
- **[uxmDailyRewards](minecraft/plugins/uxmdailyrewards/)** - One reward a day from a calendar, with streaks
- **[uxmEasterEggs](minecraft/plugins/uxmeastereggs/)** - Eggs hidden around the map for players to hunt, with rewards and leaderboards
- **[uxmEnchants](minecraft/plugins/uxmenchants/)** - Custom enchantments, one file each, in the enchanting table, trades, loot, the anvil and books
- **[uxmEssentials](minecraft/plugins/uxmessentials/)** - The essentials suite: 35 modules, from homes, warps and economy to moderation, NPCs, holograms and menus
- **[uxmGlow](minecraft/plugins/uxmglow/)** - A glowing outline a player picks for themselves, in one of the 16 team colours or several in turn
- **[uxmHeads](minecraft/plugins/uxmheads/)** - A player's face drawn in chat from their skin
- **[uxmHubPvP](minecraft/plugins/uxmhubpvp/)** - Sword fights in the lobby for the players who choose them
- **[uxmJobs](minecraft/plugins/uxmjobs/)** - Jobs that pay players for breaking, placing, killing, fishing, smelting and more
- **[uxmJoinItems](minecraft/plugins/uxmjoinitems/)** - The hotbar items a lobby hands out on join, first join, respawn or world change
- **[uxmJumpPads](minecraft/plugins/uxmjumppads/)** - Jump pads that throw a player where they point
- **[uxmKotl](minecraft/plugins/uxmkotl/)** - King of the Ladder: climb to the plate, hold it, knock the others off
- **[uxmLang](minecraft/plugins/uxmlang/)** - One language per player across the server, or across a network that shares a database
- **[uxmLevels](minecraft/plugins/uxmlevels/)** - Experience and levels on as many tracks as a server wants
- **[uxmMinions](minecraft/plugins/uxmminions/)** - Small workers that mine, chop, farm, fish, fight, collect and sell on their own
- **[uxmMOTD](minecraft/plugins/uxmmotd/)** - The server list entry: description lines, the hover list and the icon, per player language
- **[uxmMultiLobby](minecraft/plugins/uxmmultilobby/)** - A menu of your lobbies with live player counts that moves a player to the one they click
- **[uxmParkour](minecraft/plugins/uxmparkour/)** - Endless parkour generated in front of the player, with themes, difficulties and leaderboards
- **[uxmPlayerHider](minecraft/plugins/uxmplayerhider/)** - A hotbar item and a command to show everybody, only the important players, or nobody
- **[uxmPlots](minecraft/plugins/uxmplots/)** - Plot worlds for creative and building servers, with a gallery, contests, renting and protection
- **[uxmQuests](minecraft/plugins/uxmquests/)** - Quests in any number of languages, dealt per player or shared, with a battle pass
- **[uxmShop](minecraft/plugins/uxmshop/)** - A server shop from a catalogue and player shops, in one plugin
- **[uxmSimpleClaims](minecraft/plugins/uxmsimpleclaims/)** - Chunk claims with roles, flags, sub areas with rent, a market, warps, taxes and time limits
- **[uxmSkills](minecraft/plugins/uxmskills/)** - Skills that level as players mine, fight, farm, fish and more
- **[uxmTeleportBow](minecraft/plugins/uxmteleportbow/)** - A bow whose arrow carries the player to where it lands
- **[uxmClaims](minecraft/plugins/uxmclaims/getting-started/welcome.md)** - Land protection system with GUI menus
- **[uxmHelperNPC](minecraft/plugins/uxmhelpernpc/)** - A personal NPC assistant with menus, actions, holograms & an AI assistant
- **[uxmSpawners](minecraft/plugins/uxmspawners/)** - Spawners that store what they produce, upgrade, stack boosts & sell from a menu
- **[uxmFarmer](minecraft/plugins/uxmfarmer/)** - A hired farmhand NPC that harvests, kills, collects XP & sells, with per-product levels
- **[uxmDiscordSync](minecraft/plugins/uxmdiscordsync/)** - Account linking, rank sync, chat bridge, Discord-side 2FA & level rewards
- **[uxmDragonEvent](minecraft/plugins/uxmdragonevent/)** - A scheduled Ender Dragon fight with rebirths, damage leaderboards & rewards
- **[uxmBlacksmith](minecraft/plugins/uxmblacksmith/)** - Time-gated crafting with burn risk, trade slots, boosts, levels, masteries & a perk tree
- **[uxmSupplyRush](minecraft/plugins/uxmsupplyrush/)** - Timed item tournaments where players deposit items for points and the top contributors win rewards

Open-source developer tooling.

- **[uxmLib](minecraft/libraries/uxmlib/)** - A modular, Folia-ready toolkit for Paper 1.21+ plugins on Java 21: GUIs, items, commands, config, storage, integrations & HUD

## Roblox

Drop-in Luau systems for Roblox experiences. Each one is a single folder you place in
`ServerScriptService`; it installs itself on the first server start.

- **[How a UXR system works](roblox/systems/platform/)** - The bootstrap, the folders, the remotes and the profiles every system shares
- **[All systems](roblox/systems/)** - Sixteen systems: daily rewards, levels, seven games, vehicles, doors, handcuffs, pets, trolls, overheads and an admin panel

Alongside them: [Setups](roblox/setups/) and [Graphic & UI](roblox/graphic-ui/).
