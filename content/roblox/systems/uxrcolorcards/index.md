The colour-and-number shedding game. Match the top card by colour or number, play action
cards to skip, reverse or make somebody draw, and call out when you are down to your last
card.

Two to four players per table. Empty seats can be filled by bots.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| Tables | Any number, found in a world folder |
| Players | Two to four, seats claimed by sitting down |
| Deck | 108 cards, the standard composition |
| Bots | Three difficulties, filling empty seats |
| House rules | Six switches, four of them implemented |
| Turn timer | Configurable, with a last-card window |
| Scoring | Wins, losses and points |
| Leaderboards | In-world boards, global across servers |
| Card art | 63 card images ship with the system |

## The name

The product is called Color Cards and the cards are described by colour and function:
skip, reverse, draw two, wild, wild draw four. No trademarked name appears anywhere in it.

Keep it that way if you re-skin the deck. The rules of a card game are not protectable; a
name and a look are.
